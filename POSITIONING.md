# Conduikt — Positioning

**Last updated:** 2026-06-28
**Status:** Living doc. Revise when reality contradicts it.

This is the spine. Every marketing decision (homepage copy, tweets, ads, onboarding, sales calls) should trace back to one of the answers below. If a decision can't be justified against this doc, either the decision is off-strategy or the doc needs updating — pick one explicitly.

---

## 1. Who this is for

**Primary ICP:** Solo SaaS founders running their own marketing. Typically 0–10 paying customers, no marketer on the team, no budget to hire one. They are technical or semi-technical, ship the product themselves, and treat marketing as the part of the job they're worst at.

**Secondary:** Early-stage SaaS teams (2–5 people) where marketing is split across engineers and founders. Same pain, slightly more budget.

**Explicitly not for:**
- Enterprise marketing teams (they have specialists and budget for HubSpot)
- Agencies servicing many clients (multi-tenant needs we're not building for)
- E-commerce, content publishers, B2C consumer apps (different playbooks)
- Founders who already love marketing (we solve for the ones who don't)

---

## 2. What we solve

You're shipping the product *and* running marketing in your spare hours. You've stitched together five tools — a writer, a scheduler, an analytics dashboard, an SEO checker, an email tool — and none of them talk to each other. Output is inconsistent. Measurement is manual. You never actually know if marketing is working because nothing closes the loop between what you publish and what moves the numbers.

The pain isn't "I can't write copy." It's "I don't have a marketing team, and stitching together AI tools to fake one is making me a worse founder, not a better marketer."

---

## 3. The wedge

**End-to-end and opinionated.**

Audit → Generate → Publish → Analyze → Improve, in one product, with views about what good looks like.

Competitors stop at "generate." We close the loop, and the loop has *opinions* — calibration matters, ship measurable content, dark UI for serious operators, transparency over hype, build in public over performative growth.

The opinions are the moat. Generic tools become commodities. Opinionated ones become brands.

---

## 4. What we are not

- **Not a generic AI writer** — Jasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic do that better at scale
- **Not a "marketing OS" or "all-in-one platform"** — those are scope words, we sell depth
- **Not channel-agnostic** — we have opinions about which channels matter for SaaS (and which don't yet)
- **Not building for enterprise teams or agencies** — wrong shape of product
- **Not promising self-improvement without showing the receipts** — until the loop demonstrably closes on real data, we don't pitch it as the headline

---

## 5. Voice — say / don't say

| We say | We don't say |
|---|---|
| "The AI marketing team for solo SaaS founders" | "All-in-one marketing platform" |
| "Ships measurable content" | "AI-powered marketing" |
| "Opinionated workflows" | "Customizable to your needs" |
| "Closes the loop" | "End-to-end automation" |
| "For founders who haven't hired a marketer yet" | "For growing companies" |
| Concrete numbers (open rates, signups, deltas) | "10x your marketing" |
| Honest about what doesn't work yet | "Revolutionize", "transform" |

## 6. Proof points to build (in order)

1. **Loop-closure demo** — before/after engagement chart from running the feedback loop on @olaDmenace for 14 days. *(In progress — internal prototype starting 2026-06-28.)*
2. **First paying customer testimonial** — quote + their specific metric, not a vibe.
3. **A signature workflow nobody else has** — pick one and make it undeniable, instead of advertising fifteen.
4. **Public dogfooding** — Conduikt's own marketing visibly run by Conduikt. The product is the proof.

---

## 7. Revisit triggers

Update this doc when any of the following happens:

- Buyer turns out to be agencies, not founders → revise ICP (section 1)
- Self-improving loop doesn't close in real testing → drop it from positioning, find the new wedge
- A competitor (Buffer, Customer.io, Lindy, Gumloop) launches a closed-loop product → sharpen wedge against theirs specifically
- First 10 paying customers tell us we're solving something other than what we think → trust them, not the doc
- Geography clarifies — Lagos/Africa-first or global-SaaS-first? Currently we straddle and it shows in the marketing voice

---

## 8. Out of scope for v1 (do not chase)

- Enterprise feature requests
- White-label / agency mode
- More than the channels we already publish to
- "Self-improvement" claims beyond what the loop actually demonstrates
- Sales-to-acquisition story (zero MRR is too early to position for exit — and acquirers see through it)
