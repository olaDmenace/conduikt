# The marketing stack is broken for SMBs

<div class="lead">

SMB marketers and agencies spend <span class="stat">60–80%</span> of their time on repetitive content and campaign work.

</div>

The problem isn't AI quality. It's **fragmentation**.

- A writer in one tool, a scheduler in another, an audit in a third
- Performance data lives in a fourth, never feeds back into the first
- 10+ apps stitched with manual handoffs
- Output quality is inconsistent because data doesn't flow between them

The result: founders running their own marketing burn hours on context-switching instead of building product.

<Footer />

---

# One platform. <span class="brand">18 agents.</span> A closed loop.

<div class="loop">

**Audit** → **Generate** → **Publish** → **Analyze** → **Improve**

</div>

- **18 specialised agents** today — SEO audit, content strategy, copywriting, social, email sequences, campaigns, A/B tests, programmatic SEO, growth playbooks, more
- Agents share research, brand voice, and performance data — no patchwork
- The **closed-loop analytics layer** shipped in Week 2 — engagement data automatically informs next week's generation. The loop tightens as each channel accumulates signal
- Built for the founder who is also their own marketer — the buyer enterprise tools ignore
