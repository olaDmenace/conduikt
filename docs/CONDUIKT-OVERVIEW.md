# Conduikt
### AI Marketing Automation, in plain English

One platform. One operator. The work of a marketing team — done by AI.

---

## What it is

Conduikt is an AI-powered marketing platform that helps anyone — founders, small businesses, agencies — **plan, create, schedule, and publish marketing content** across multiple channels, all from one place.

You paste a website URL. Conduikt does the rest.

---

## What it does

1. **Researches your business** — competitors, keywords, audience, brand voice.
2. **Plans your marketing** — 30/60/90-day calendar with KPIs.
3. **Creates the content** — blog posts, social posts, emails, ad copy, short-form video.
4. **Publishes everywhere** — LinkedIn, X, TikTok, email, your own blog.
5. **Measures performance** — pulls live numbers from Google Analytics and Search Console.

---

## What you can make

- **Blog posts** — SEO-optimised, 1500 words, with images.
- **Social posts** — LinkedIn, X, TikTok-ready.
- **Emails** — broadcasts and drip sequences.
- **Video ads** — short-form vertical video with an AI presenter.
- **Landing pages & ad copy** — conversion-focused.
- **Programmatic SEO pages** — generate dozens of targeted pages in one click.

---

## How it works (at a glance)

- A team of **specialised AI agents** — one per skill (SEO, copy, social, video, etc.)
- A **scheduler** that publishes posts at the right time on the right platform
- A **measurement layer** that feeds results back into the next round of content
- A **simple dashboard** — no AI prompting required, just clicks

---

## Who it's for

- **Solo founders** who can't afford a marketing team
- **Small businesses** that want consistent output without an agency retainer
- **Marketing agencies** managing many clients at once
- **Course creators, coaches, and local businesses** who need to show up daily

---

## Why it matters

- **One operator** can do the work of a 5–9 person marketing team.
- **Content output goes from monthly to daily** — without burning out.
- **Performance compounds** — the system learns what works and does more of it.
